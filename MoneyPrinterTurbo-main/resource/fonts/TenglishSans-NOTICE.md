# Tenglish Sans

`TenglishSans-Regular.ttf` and `TenglishSans-Bold.ttf` are merged fonts for
subtitles that mix Telugu and English (Tenglish):

- Telugu glyphs and shaping tables: **Noto Sans Telugu** (Regular / Bold),
  Copyright 2019-2022 Google LLC.
- Latin letters, digits and ASCII punctuation: **Be Vietnam Pro** (Medium / Bold),
  Copyright 2021 The Be Vietnam Pro Project Authors (https://github.com/bettergui/BeVietnamPro).

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). The merged fonts are distributed under the same
license with the new family name "Tenglish Sans".
